01 // About

Built for the Machine Shop
and the Sim Lab

I'm a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at JSPM's Rajarshi Shahu College of Engineering, Pune (B.Tech, 2024–2028), with hands-on experience across the full design-to-manufacturing workflow — CAD design, structural analysis, CFD, and machining.

As a Structures Domain Engineer at Team Red Line Racing, I design and validate structural components for our Formula Student car — the one that took All India Rank 1 at Formula Imperial 2025. My work spans manufacturing-ready CATIA V5 models, chassis analysis in ANSYS Static Structural, and coordinating with fabrication teams so what we simulate is what we can actually build.

I've also spent time on a real shop floor at Harihar Industries, working with CNC, VMC, and SPMC machines — because I believe good designers understand how their parts get made. I'm aiming for R&D, design, and CAE-focused roles.
